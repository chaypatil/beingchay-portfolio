      Six steps from a comment to a booked call.

      AI does the reading, sorting and first drafts. A person still sends every message, because that's where trust lives.

        A post goes live

        Claude reads the comment and profile and marks it hot, warm, cold or skip. Spam and vendor pitches get filtered out.
